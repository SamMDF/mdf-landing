Accessibility Statement

We want this site to be usable by as many people as possible. Our reference target is the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA.

Measures in place

  • Semantic structure with a heading hierarchy and landmarks.
  • A “Skip to content” link and a visible focus indicator on every interactive element.
  • Modal dialogs trap and restore keyboard focus and close with the Escape key.
  • Color contrast checked against level AA thresholds.
  • The system “reduce motion” preference is respected: animations are disabled.
  • Alternative text on meaningful images; decorative images are marked as such.

Known limitations

  • The Vimeo video player and the embedded experience are third-party components whose accessibility we do not fully control.
  • Fonts are served from an external provider (fonts.cdnfonts.com).

How to report a problem

If you find an accessibility barrier, email cesar@maestrosdelfuturo.com and describe the problem and the page. We respond and try to fix it as soon as possible.
